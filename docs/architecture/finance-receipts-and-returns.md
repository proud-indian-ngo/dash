# Finance: receipts and the donations return

Donation receipts and their numbering, and the donations return (Form 113/114) readiness page and export.

Donor records in [finance-income.md](finance-income.md); overview in [finance-and-programmes.md](finance-and-programmes.md).

## Donation receipts and numbering

Rules start in `packages/shared/src/receipts.ts`.

- Policy (founder decision): Razorpay and give.do send their own 80G receipts (give.do under the trust's registration), so the dashboard receipts only direct gifts (UPI, NEFT, cheque, cash) received from 1 April 2026. In-kind gifts are not receipted.
- One live receipt per gift. A server-only snapshot freezes what the PDF prints, so later edits never change an issued receipt.
- Numbering (`PI/2026-27/0001`): one gap-free series per financial year of the issue date, from a locked counter row in the issuing transaction, so a rollback gives the number back; a Postgres `SEQUENCE` would skip. Voiding keeps the number.
- Eligibility: direct gift on or after the start, `received`, no refund, INR, not a platform donor, donor with name and address with PIN. The 80G line (needing a valid ID, printed in full) is left out when the gift is excluded, the donor opted out, or cash is above ₹2,000; the receipt then says no deduction certificate is issued. The trust's particulars and 80G registration must be set first.
- `donors.receipt` (finance admins and super admins) issues, voids with a reason and emails. Issuing sets the gift `receipted`; voiding returns it to `received`. Reading also needs an income permission. Every fetch of the PDF, including opening it in the viewer, is audited as a download because it carries the full ID.
- The PDF is rendered once and stored in R2 with its SHA-256; downloads and emails serve that file, so an issued receipt never changes, even when the template does. Issuing queues `store-contribution-receipt-pdf` per receipt; a fetch before it runs (or of a receipt issued before storing) stores it the same way. A voided receipt is rendered afresh with the VOID marker; the stored original stays. Email is manual only, through a job with an idempotency key.
- The signatory's signature prints above their name. Finance uploads a photo or scan in Settings; the browser makes the paper transparent and fits it to the payee-signature PNG limits, which the server checks on claim. Only `appConfig.saveReceiptSettings` writes the receipt settings, because it alone sets the signature key from an upload it claims. Each snapshot freezes the key, so a replaced signature never changes issued receipts; both settings and snapshots count as references for R2 cleanup. Receipts issued before signatures print the name alone. No rule found requires a signature on the trust's own receipt (CA to confirm); it is there because donors and reviewers expect one, and proves nothing on its own.
- Every receipt carries a QR code that opens a public page saying whether Proud Indian issued it and whether it was voided. The page shows what a forger would change (amount, mode, the 80G line, the trust's PAN and URN) and the donor only as initials, never the ID or address, so a leaked link shows little. Refunds aren't shown: only gateway gifts are refunded, and those are never receipted here.
- The link is an HMAC of the receipt ID under a key derived from `DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, on the `APP_URL` origin. Both are printed on paper for good: the key never changes, and the origin must keep serving or redirecting `/verify/r/*`. A bad signature reads as an unknown receipt. The page also checks a PDF copy against the stored hash in the browser, so the file never leaves the device; any re-save fails it, so it reports a different file, not a forgery. The PDF is not digitally signed: a printed copy loses the signature and a self-signed one shows as untrusted, while the QR survives printing.
- Receipts have no ledger effect.

## Donations return (Form 113/114)

Form 113 (Income-tax Act 2025, formerly Form 10BD) is due by 31 May 2027 for FY 2026-27; Form 114 certificates (formerly 10BE) are generated by the portal afterwards. Gifts before 1 April 2026 were filed outside the dashboard. Rules, codes and columns live in one module, `packages/shared/src/donations-return.ts`, used by the readiness page, the "Missing donor details" stat and the export.

### Inclusion rules

Every received gift of the year is a candidate, any channel (give.do gifts go in under each real donor). In order:

- Waiting: status `pending`.
- Excluded: reversed, fully refunded, foreign currency, in kind (CA to confirm), cash above ₹2,000 per donation, marked Exclude, or from an opted-out donor (gift on Follow donor). In kind and cash are judged by channel as well as mode.
- In the return, ready unless blocked by: a platform such as GiveIndia recorded as donor, or a donor without a name, an address (line 1 and PIN code), or a valid ID (PAN shape, or 5–20 alphanumerics for other documents; undecryptable counts as invalid).
- A partly refunded gift is reported at its net amount (CA to confirm).

### Readiness page and export

- The readiness page lists what blocks filing, including CSR gifts for the CA to decide. Without `DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY` IDs are unchecked and the upload file is refused.
- The export needs `finance.view_sensitive` and is audited because it reveals identifiers in bulk. It writes either the portal upload file (one row per donor, donation type and mode, each cash gift its own row; ready gifts only; net amounts in rupees) or a CA file of every gift with status and reason, ID masked.
- Codes are written as full text. Voluntary and CSR map to "Specific grant", matching the FY 2025-26 filing. The official Form 113 template could not be verified, so columns follow the FY 2025-26 Form 10BD; check against the portal before each upload.
- The 80G registration (URN, date, section code) is entered in Settings, never seeded. The upload file is refused until it is set.
