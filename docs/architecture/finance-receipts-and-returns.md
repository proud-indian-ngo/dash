# Finance: receipts and the donations return

Donation receipts and their numbering, and the donations return (Form 113/114) readiness page and export.

Donor records in [finance-income.md](finance-income.md); overview in [finance-and-programmes.md](finance-and-programmes.md).

## Donation receipts and numbering

Code: `packages/shared/src/receipts.ts`, `packages/db/src/schema/contribution-receipt.ts`, `packages/db/src/queries/contribution-receipts.ts`, `apps/web/src/functions/contribution-receipts.ts`, `packages/pdf/src/donation-receipt.tsx`.

- Policy (founder decision): Razorpay and give.do send their own 80G receipts (give.do under the trust's registration), so the dashboard receipts only direct gifts (`upi`, `neft`, `cheque`, `cash`) received from `RECEIPTING_START_DAY` = 2026-04-01. In-kind gifts are not receipted.
- `contribution_receipt` (synced to `finance.view`, `finance.manage`, `donors.manage`, `donors.receipt`): number `PI/2026-27/0001`, issue date, void columns, `emailed_at`. One live receipt per gift. Server-only `contribution_receipt_snapshot` freezes what the PDF prints, so later edits never change an issued receipt.
- Numbering: one gap-free series per financial year of the issue date, from a locked counter row (`contribution_receipt_counter`, `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE … RETURNING`) in the issuing transaction, so a rollback gives the number back; a Postgres `SEQUENCE` would skip. Voiding keeps the number.
- Eligibility (`assessReceipt`): direct gift on or after the start, `received`, no refund, INR, not a platform donor, donor with name and address with PIN. The 80G line (needing a valid ID, printed in full) is left out when the gift is excluded, the donor opted out, or cash above ₹2,000; the receipt then says no deduction certificate is issued. The trust's particulars (`app_config.donation_receipt_settings`) and 80G registration must be set first.
- `donors.receipt` (finance admins and super admins) issues (up to 500 at a time, numbered in received-date order, gift set to `receipted`), voids with a reason (gift back to `received`) and emails, audited as `contribution_receipt.issue`, `.void`, `.email`. Reading also needs an income permission (`canReadReceipts`). Download (`GET /api/receipts/$receiptId`) is audited because the PDF carries the full ID.
- The PDF is rendered on demand, never stored. Email is manual only, through the `send-contribution-receipt` job with an idempotency key.
- The Receipts tab and the Receipt column (`receiptColumnValue`) show readiness; the split of open direct gifts comes from `getContributionReceiptReadiness` (gift IDs and block codes only). Receipts have no ledger effect.

## Donations return (Form 113/114)

Form 113 (Income-tax Act 2025, formerly Form 10BD) is due by 31 May 2027 for FY 2026-27; Form 114 certificates (formerly 10BE) are generated by the portal afterwards. Gifts before 1 April 2026 (`FIRST_RETURN_DAY`) were filed outside the dashboard. Rules, codes and columns live in one module, `packages/shared/src/donations-return.ts`, used by the readiness page, the "Missing donor details" stat and the export.

### Inclusion rules

Every received gift of the year is a candidate, any channel (give.do gifts go in under each real donor). In order:

- Waiting: status `pending`.
- Excluded: reversed, fully refunded, foreign currency, in kind (CA to confirm), cash above ₹2,000 per donation (`CASH_DEDUCTION_LIMIT_PAISE`), marked Exclude, or from an opted-out donor (gift on Follow donor). In kind and cash are judged by channel as well as mode (`effectiveReportingMode`).
- In the return, ready unless blocked by: a platform such as GiveIndia recorded as donor, or a donor without a name, an address (line 1 and PIN code, `hasReturnAddress`), or a valid ID (PAN shape, or 5–20 alphanumerics for other documents; undecryptable counts as invalid).
- A partly refunded gift is reported at its net amount (CA to confirm).

### Readiness page and export

- `/income/return` (four income permissions; FY 2026-27 onward) shows counts, progress and a checklist of what blocks filing, including CSR gifts for the CA to decide. The server returns IDs, outcomes and amounts only. Without `DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY` IDs are unchecked and the upload file is refused.
- `exportDonationsReturn` (`finance.view_sensitive`, audited `donations_return.export` because it reveals identifiers in bulk) writes either the portal upload file (one row per donor, donation type code and mode code, each cash gift its own row; ready gifts only; net amounts in rupees; no BOM or apostrophe guard) or "All gifts (for your CA)" (every gift with status and reason, ID masked).
- Codes are written as full text. Voluntary and CSR map to "Specific grant" (`VOLUNTARY_DONATION_TYPE_CODE`, `CSR_DONATION_TYPE_CODE`), matching the FY 2025-26 filing. The official Form 113 template could not be verified, so columns follow the FY 2025-26 Form 10BD; the page says to check against the portal before each upload.
- The 80G registration (URN, date, section code) is `app_config.donations_return_registration`, entered in Settings → Organisation accounts (`finance.manage`), never seeded. The upload file is refused until it is set.
